Support

DupeFont identifies the typography in an image and ranks the closest free, openly licensed look-alikes. If something is wrong or you have a question, we'd like to hear about it.

Contact us

Email {{ config.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}. There is no account to sign in to and no ticket number to quote — just describe what happened. We read every message.

Reporting a wrong match

Matches are visual estimates, not identifications: DupeFont finds free fonts that look like the type in your image, and it does not claim to name the original. If a result looks wrong, the fastest way to tell us is the “Was this right?” prompt shown beneath any image result — it attaches the match we produced so we can reproduce it. You can also email us a description, or a share link if you created one.

The DupeFont app for ChatGPT

The app connects ChatGPT to the same matching service that powers this site. If it stops responding in a chat, check the website first: if matching works here, the problem is with the connection rather than the matcher, and reconnecting the app in ChatGPT usually clears it. Tell us either way.

Licensing questions

Every match shows the licence we hold for that font. We report what the font's own metadata says; where a font ships without usable licence metadata we show its licence as unknown rather than guess. Always check the licence that ships with a font before using it in a product.

Privacy and terms

For what we store and for how long, see the Privacy Policy. For the rules that apply when you use the Service, see the Terms of Service. Privacy questions can go to privacy@dupefont.com and legal notices to legal@dupefont.com.
